Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Overview

Mosaic Quest is an Android game published by adrianobr. This policy describes how the game and the Google services it uses handle data. The game has no account, registration form or developer-operated server.

Data stored on your device

The game saves unlocked stages, first-time rewards, coins, hints, magic wands and purchase tokens in the app's private storage on your device. A purchase token is kept to avoid granting the same consumable pack twice. These records are not sent to an adrianobr server. The game does not request access to your photos, contacts or precise location.

Ads and data sent to Google

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to load optional rewarded video ads. Google may receive your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad or app identifiers, interactions such as app launches and video views, and diagnostic data. Google uses this data for ads, measurement and fraud prevention according to its own policies. The SDK can process data when the game initializes it, even if you do not choose to watch a video. This app does not currently provide its own consent or privacy-options screen.

Purchases

Optional hint-and-wand packs are purchased through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and may handle payment and account data under its policies; Mosaic Quest does not receive your payment card details. The app receives the product ID, purchase status, quantity and token needed to deliver the items. It stores the token and item balance locally. Consumable balances are not synchronized with an adrianobr account and may not be restored after reinstalling or changing devices.

Retention and security

Local game records remain until you clear the app data or remove the app. An Android or Google device backup may retain and restore some app data depending on your device settings. adrianobr does not keep a separate server copy. App-private Android storage limits access by other apps. Data sent to Google by its services is encrypted in transit according to Google; Google determines its own storage and retention periods.

Your choices

You can clear local game records in Android Settings > Apps > Mosaic Quest > Storage > Clear storage. You can manage or delete the Android advertising ID in your device privacy settings. Watching an ad and purchasing a pack are optional. For requests about data handled by Google, consult Google's privacy and payments policies. If you have questions about data handled by adrianobr, email the contact below.

Contact and updates

Publisher: adrianobr. Privacy contact: contact@adrianobr.com. This policy may change when the app, ads or payments change. The updated date at the top will reflect a new version.

设备本地保存的数据

游戏在设备上的应用私有存储空间保存已解锁关卡、首次通关奖励、金币、提示、魔法棒及购买令牌。保存令牌是为了防止重复发放消耗型礼包。这些记录不会发送到 adrianobr 的服务器。游戏不会请求访问您的照片、联系人或精确位置。

广告及发送至 Google 的数据

游戏使用 Google Mobile Ads SDK 加载可选的激励视频广告。Google 可能接收 IP 地址（可推断大致位置）、广告或应用标识符、应用启动和视频观看等交互信息以及诊断数据。Google 依据其政策将这些数据用于广告、效果衡量及防欺诈。即使您不观看视频，SDK 在游戏初始化时也可能处理数据。目前应用没有内置独立的同意或隐私选项界面。

购买

可选的提示与魔法棒礼包通过 Google Play Billing 购买。Google 根据其政策处理付款，也可能处理付款和账号数据；Mosaic Quest 不会收到您的银行卡信息。应用接收发放道具所需的商品 ID、购买状态、数量和令牌，并在本地保存令牌与道具余额。消耗型道具余额不与 adrianobr 账号同步，重装或更换设备后可能无法恢复。

保留与安全

本地记录会保留到您清除应用数据或卸载应用。Android 或 Google 的设备备份可能根据您的设置保留并恢复部分数据。adrianobr 不另行在服务器保存副本。Android 应用私有存储限制其他应用访问。根据 Google 的说明，发送给其服务的数据在传输中加密；Google 自行确定这些服务的数据保存期限。

您的选择

您可以在 Android 设置 > 应用 > Mosaic Quest > 存储 > 清除数据中删除本地记录，也可以在设备的隐私设置中管理或删除广告 ID。观看广告和购买礼包均为自愿。如需处理 Google 所持数据，请查阅 Google 的隐私及付款政策。如对 adrianobr 处理的数据有疑问，请使用下方邮箱联系。
