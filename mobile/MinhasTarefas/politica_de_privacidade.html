English

Privacy policy. Minhas Tarefas helps you create tasks, schedule reminders, use a widget, and personalize the appearance of the app.

Data. Tasks, summaries, dates, times, completion status, appearance preferences, and a background image selected by you are stored locally on your device. The app has no account, login, cloud sync, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or sale of personal data. We do not collect or share your personal information.

Permissions and your choices. Notification permission is used only to send task reminders. Exact alarms may be used to deliver reminders at the scheduled time, and boot permission is used only to restore future reminders after restarting the device. You may disable permissions or delete the app and its data in Android settings. For questions, contact the developer through the support channel shown in the Google Play listing.
